# Monitor

The apps' main mode: public videos from official accounts (governments, heads of state,
ministries, parliaments, the UN family, EU institutions, NATO, the IMF, the World Bank...), from the
accounts tapacuk93 follows, from the authors of the owner's Instagram reposts, and from what the
platforms recommend next to those. Every video is transcribed on the box, and a **hit** is
flagged when:

- **name:** someone says a word that sounds like one of the owner's phrases (today `тарас`,
  `тарас маслов`, `маслов`) in any grammatical form, transliteration or likely mishearing; or
- **language:** Ukrainian or Russian is spoken for a few seconds in a source that does not normally
  speak it.

Every hit carries the excerpt (and an English translation), the time in the video, a short audio
clip and a frame, the scores it was built from and notes on why it may be a coincidence. Nothing is
ever presented as certain: hits are `likely` (>= 0.7), `possible` (>= 0.4) or `low confidence`,
and low-confidence ones are kept on purpose (recall over precision). The API and the Hit shape are
in `../CONTRACT.md` ("Monitor").

## How it works

```
sources.json (official, verified)  ─┐
owner-added (POST /v1/monitor/sources)│      ┌─ telegram: t.me/s/<channel> preview (public, no login)
tapacuk93's follows (IG, FB)          ├─► poll ┼─ facebook: the downloader account's browser session
reposts (backend items.repost_of)     │      ├─ instagram: the collector's browser session (when logged in)
recommendations around seeds  ───────┘      └─ youtube: channel RSS feed / Data API (download: see below)
        │
        ▼  queued (requested first, then official/followed/reposted, then recommended - own quota)
   download (audio or the smallest progressive MP4) ─► ffmpeg 16 kHz mono ─► Silero VAD chunks <= 20 s
        ─► faster-whisper small int8, per chunk: language probabilities + words with timestamps
        ─► detect: phrases (fuzzy/phonetic, Latin+Cyrillic) and uk/ru-where-unexpected
        ─► Claude (aicoin): English translation + a name-vs-common-word opinion on borderline hits
        ─► hits (+ <= 20 s MP3 clip and a frame on media.empire.earth) ─► media deleted, transcript kept
```

- **Sources** (`eemonitor/registry.py`): `sources.json` is data - 101 entities, 330 accounts on
  YouTube, Telegram, X, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, VK, Rutube - each verified (YouTube by its
  channel feed's title, Telegram by its preview's title, the rest copied from the official site;
  how, per entry, is in the file's `method`, `verification` and `note` fields; what could not be
  verified is listed under `unverified`). Each source has `expected_languages`; for followed,
  reposted and recommended sources, which have none, they are inferred from scans (languages
  making up a quarter of the speech), and until then a video's own main language is used.
- **Phrases** (`eemonitor/phrases.py`): variants are generated by rule - Ukrainian and Russian
  case forms (consonant-final names: Тарас, Тараса, Тарасу, Тарасові, Тарасом, Тарасі, Тарасе,
  diminutives; -ов/-ев/-ин surnames in all genders and numbers: Маслов, Маслова, Маслову,
  Масловим/Масловым, Маслові/Маслове, Маслових/Масловых...), multi-word phrases declined together
  (Тараса Маслова, Тарасові Маслову), transliterations (Taras, Tarasa, Maslov, Maslow, Masloff)
  and mishearings (an unstressed vowel changed, a doubled consonant, final v/f, s/z). When a phrase
  is saved Claude may add more spellings once; they are cached. A changed list applies to new
  scans, and `POST /v1/monitor/rescan` matches every stored transcript again without downloading.
- **Matching** (`eemonitor/detect.py`): each transcribed word is reduced to one Latin form, then
  scored against the phrase's variants: exact form (0.9-0.95), Claude spelling (0.6), mishearing
  (0.55), a word built on it (Tarasov, Tarasivka: 0.5), one letter off (0.5), same sound key (0.35),
  known look-alike words (terrace, Taurus, tiaras: 0.15-0.4). Confidence = sound x (0.55 + 0.45 x
  Whisper's word probability) x name-vs-word (0.85 exact, 0.6 fuzzy, lower for look-alikes after
  "the/on the"; Claude's opinion replaces it on borderline hits). Notes say e.g. "refers to Taras
  Shevchenko", "Maslow is also an English surname", "used as a common noun".
- **Language mismatch:** consecutive chunks identified as uk or ru (p >= 0.35, East Slavic >= 0.45,
  at least 3 s of speech, not Whisper's no-speech or low-probability output) in a source whose
  expected languages exclude that language. The confidence is weighted by the letters in the
  transcript (і/ї/є/ґ vs ы/э/ъ/ё) and by duration, with a note when Whisper's ru/uk probabilities
  are close - it confuses the two - and a reminder that it may be a quote, an interpreter or a song.

## Platforms

| Platform | Listing | Media | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Telegram | `t.me/s/<channel>`: Telegram's public preview, no login | the preview's `<video src>`; for videos too large for the preview, Telegram's API (Telethon, MIT) with the owner's session | **live**. The API route starts once the owner has run `monitor/deploy/telegram-login.py` on the Mac (phone and code typed by the owner; the session string goes to `earth-empire/telegram-session`). Public channels only, FloodWait honoured, at most `telegram_api.daily_downloads` (60) a day |
| Facebook | the downloader account's session (`earth-empire/facebook-dl-session`), page Videos and Reels tabs; Facebook's own GraphQL JSON | the progressive MP4 URL in that JSON | **live**. The owner's own account (`earth-empire/facebook-session`) is never used here |
| Instagram | the collector's browser and session (`collector/eecollector/igweb.py`, imported, same profile lock) | the reel's video URL | **waits for the collector's login**; checks again every 3 h, then starts by itself (following list, reels, reposts' surroundings) |
| Reposts | backend `items.repost_of` (read-only from the backend DB) | the copy already on `media.empire.earth` | **live** (scanned without touching Instagram); recommendations around them wait for Instagram |
| YouTube | channel RSS feed (no key), or Data API v3 if `earth-empire/youtube` `{"api_key"}` exists | yt-dlp | **off** (`youtube.enabled=0`): YouTube answers the box's AWS address with "Sign in to confirm you're not a bot" |
| X | API v2 (`earth-empire/x`) | the video variant URL | **off** (`x.enabled=0`): pay-per-use with a $0 balance; billing refusals (402 / "credits") stop it, they are never retried |
| TikTok, VK, Rutube | listed in the registry | - | not fetched (see below) |

**What is possible on the others.** TikTok has no public API for other accounts' videos (the
Research API is for approved academics); its web app needs heavy anti-bot handling - not
implemented. VK has an official API (`video.get` with a service token) that returns player links,
not files; Rutube has an undocumented public JSON API with HLS streams - both possible later with
yt-dlp's extractors. YouTube's Data API gives listings and caption *tracks* (an auto-caption track
in `ru` on an English channel is itself a signal) but no media and, for others' videos, no caption
text.

**yt-dlp trade-off.** yt-dlp (Unlicense) reads public videos the way a browser does. That is
against YouTube's terms for automated downloading, so it is used only for YouTube, only for public
videos, audio only, deleted after transcription - and today not at all, since YouTube refuses the
box. Options, each the owner's choice: a residential relay (the same pipeline run from a home
machine), YouTube cookies of a throwaway account (more against the terms), or staying with
listings plus captions metadata.

**Pacing** (`eemonitor/pace.py`): a random gap between requests to one platform (Telegram 4-10 s,
Facebook 8-22 s, Instagram 8-20 s on top of the collector's own 7-18 s and hourly cap), a longer
pause every 15-25 requests, daily request caps per platform (`<platform>.daily_requests`),
exponential backoff on 429/5xx (1, 5, 20 min), and a **stop** recorded in `stops` (shown in
`/v1/monitor/status` and the backend's `/v1/status`) on any checkpoint, login page, warning text or
billing refusal, with a screenshot in `/var/lib/earth-empire/monitor/screenshots/`. Facebook types
the downloader's password only when its session is logged out, once; any challenge stops it.
`ee-monitor resume <platform>` clears a stop.

**Recommendations:** seeds are the owner's reposts first, then hits with confidence >= 0.25. Around
a seed: Instagram's reels viewer started at that reel (the reels it plays next) and the author's
other reels; Facebook's watch/reel page suggestions. Found videos are `recommended`, `via` the
seed, `hop` + 1 (at most 2), at most 8 per seed and 20 seeds a day, scanned within their own
60-minutes-a-day quota so official sources are never starved.

## Cost and capacity

Measured on the box (t4g.medium, 2 Graviton2 vCPU, int8, beam 1), RTF = processing time / audio:

| Model | RTF (83 s Russian clip) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| tiny | 0.11-0.43 | poor Russian; hallucinates on music |
| base | 0.14 | usable language ID, weaker words |
| small | 0.42 (in the pipeline: see live numbers below) | used: good uk/ru/en words and language ID |

- **CPU on the box (chosen):** no extra cost - the box is paid for. At RTF ~0.5 and `Nice=10` a day
  holds roughly 24-40 h of audio in theory; the default cap is 240 min/day (+60 recommended) so the
  API, the collector and the browsers keep room. Memory: whisper small ~1 GB, Chromium ~0.8 GB.
- **On-demand GPU:** g4dn.xlarge (T4) $0.526/h; faster-whisper large-v3 runs ~20-30x real time
  there, so ~$0.02-0.03 per hour of audio plus start-up and idle time; worth it only for a backlog
  of many hours (needs approval: paid).
- **API:** OpenAI whisper-1 through the aicoin proxy at $0.006/min = $0.36 per hour of audio, one
  language per file (no per-chunk language ID), every call at least one coin. Not used.
- **Claude** (translations and borderline judgements): one call per video with hits, capped at
  `claude.daily_usd` (0.50).

## On the box

`deploy/install.sh` (run by `backend/deploy/deploy-box.sh monitor`) installs the venv
`/opt/earth-empire/monitor-venv` (this package, the backend package for secrets and aicoin, the
collector's requirements for its browser), the units `ee-monitor-api` (127.0.0.1:8010) and
`ee-monitor-worker`, the nginx routes in `/etc/nginx/earth-empire.d/monitor.conf`, and `ee-monitor`:

```sh
ee-monitor status | hits 20 | fetch 10 [platform] | poll [platform] | resume facebook
ee-monitor scan-url https://t.me/MID_Russia/94660 ru     # any public video, its expected languages
ee-monitor phrases тарас "тарас маслов" маслов            # replace the phrases
ee-monitor set scan.daily_audio_minutes 480              # caps, thresholds, whisper.model
ee-monitor bench some.mp3 small                          # transcription speed here
```

State: `/var/lib/earth-empire/monitor/monitor.sqlite3` (backed up with the backend's), models in
`models/`, screenshots of stops in `screenshots/`. Audio and video are downloaded to `tmp/` and
deleted after each scan; transcripts are kept (zlib) for rescans.

## Tests

`python -m pytest monitor/tests` - detection over hand-made transcripts (forms, mishearings,
look-alikes, multi-word phrases, language runs, uk/ru letters), the worker and API over fixtures
(saved Telegram previews, Instagram v1 media and Facebook GraphQL in their real shapes, a fake
backend DB with a repost, a fake Instagram browser for recommendations). `tests/audio/` has the
synthetic-speech end-to-end check run on the box.

## Open-source dependencies

faster-whisper (MIT) with OpenAI Whisper weights (MIT) via CTranslate2 (MIT), Silero VAD (MIT,
bundled), PyAV (BSD-3), FFmpeg (LGPL/GPL, the system package), yt-dlp (Unlicense), Playwright
(Apache-2.0) with Chromium (BSD-3), FastAPI (MIT), Uvicorn (BSD-3), httpx (BSD-3), boto3
(Apache-2.0). Claude is the one non-open component, used through the backend's existing route.
